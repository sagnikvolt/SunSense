How this is calculated
  • Size = monthly units ÷ (30 × sun hours × 0.8), capped at roof area ÷ 10 m² per kW.
  • Generation = kW × sun hours × 365 × 0.8 (inverter, wiring, heat and dust losses).
  • Cost ₹55,000 per kW installed. Subsidy ₹30,000/kW for the first 2 kW, ₹18,000 for the 3rd, capped at ₹78,000. On-grid only.
  • Savings use WBSEDCL domestic slabs: ₹3.15 / 4.25 / 5.85 / 7.15 / 8.25 per unit for 0–75 / 76–125 / 126–200 / 201–400 / 400+ units. Fixed charge and duty are left out.
  • CO₂ = generation × 0.82 kg per kWh (CEA grid baseline). The 25-year line assumes no tariff rise and 0.5% panel ageing a year.
  • Off-grid battery cost isn't included yet.
